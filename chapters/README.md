# Computational Finance (AI Plus One): chapter guide

[Return to the book overview](../README.md).

### Part 1 · Build and inspect the instrument model

1. [Specify the Financial Question Before Building the Model](../chapters/01-specify-the-financial-question.md): Define the decision, inputs, units and an independent check before modeling.
2. [Reconstruct the Return Before Scaling the Risk](../chapters/02-reconstruct-returns-and-risk.md): Reconstruct a return series before comparing annualized return and risk.
3. [Price the Claim, Not the Label](../chapters/03-price-the-specified-claim.md): Price the cash flows and ownership claim written into a contract.
4. [Separate Portfolio Value From the Fund's Trading Price](../chapters/04-separate-fund-nav-from-price.md): Distinguish portfolio net asset value from the price at which fund shares trade.
5. [Keep Time Zero Out of the Future-Value Loop](../chapters/05-keep-time-zero-out-of-the-loop.md): Handle the initial payment separately when computing future value.
6. [Separate Asset Risk From Account Risk](../chapters/06-separate-asset-and-account-risk.md): Separate an asset’s returns from the experience of an account with cash movements.
7. [Replicate an Option Before Pricing Its Story](../chapters/07-replicate-an-option-payoff.md): Replicate a simple option payoff and use the replication to check a valuation.

### Part 2 · Measure risk in portfolios

8. [Pair the Observations Before Measuring Diversification](../chapters/08-pair-the-observations.md): Pair observations consistently before calculating covariance and diversification.
9. [Build and Stress the Feasible Set](../chapters/09-build-and-stress-the-feasible-set.md): Build a feasible portfolio set and test it under different constraints.
10. [Allocate the Risky Budget, Then Rebalance the Holdings](../chapters/10-allocate-and-rebalance.md): Separate a risky budget from the rules that rebalance its holdings.
11. [Price Exposure, Not Certainty](../chapters/11-price-exposure-not-certainty.md): Estimate market exposure without passing off a fitted coefficient as certainty.

### Part 3 · Reconcile and defend the decision

12. [Connect Statements to the Forecast, Not to a Headline](../chapters/12-connect-statements-to-the-forecast.md): Reconcile a forecast to financial statements and source assumptions.
13. [Defend the Model When the Attractive Answer Fails](../chapters/13-defend-the-model-when-it-fails.md): Name a failed assumption and defend a checked decision under changed inputs.
